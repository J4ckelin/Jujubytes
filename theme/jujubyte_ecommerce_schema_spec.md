# Jujubyte E-commerce Specification & Database Schema

Based on PostgreSQL / Supabase architecture:
- Entities: categories, products, product_images, coupons, promotions, customers, orders, order_items.
- Visual style: Minimalist, Mercado Livre inspired navigation/structure, custom palette centered on Blue & Purple (#2563EB / #4F46E5 / #7C3AED / #9333EA).
- Sides:
  1. Store Administration (Lado da Loja): CRUD products & categories, promotion and coupon management, orders list/status tracking, customers list.
  2. Client Storefront (Lado do Cliente): Minimalist marketplace catalog, promotion banners, product cards with instant discounts, shopping cart & quick checkout.
